How it works

Every trade of $COVE pays creator fees that rain golden balls on the island. Real cards from our own collection roam it on a schedule.

⚡

Fees drop golden balls

Each claim scatters golden balls across the island, each with a tall light beam. Walk over one to bag it.

🃏

A real card roams

On a schedule we release one card from our collection as a glowing slab. It wanders, runs from you, and jumps around the island.

🎯

Every hit is a roll

Each golden-ball hit is a provably fair roll. Odds start tiny and climb with every miss, so anyone's hit can be the one.

🎁

The winning hit gets a one-time claim code. DM it to us on X with your address and we mail you the physical card.

Market

Trade duplicate critters with other trainers, at the bazaar at the end of the south-west path or right here.

  1. List a duplicatePick a spare critter and name the species you want back. No wallet needed.
  2. Swap instantlyWhen someone has what you want, the trade settles the moment they accept.
  3. Or sell for $COVESet a price. The buyer pays your wallet and we check the transfer on-chain.

🔒

Verify

We can't pick the winner, and you can check that yourself.

  1. CommitWhen a card is released, the server draws a random 32-byte secret and publishes sha256(secret), also in an on-chain memo.
  2. RollHit #n (index i = n − 1) wins if HMAC-SHA256(secret, "cardId:i") / 2²⁵⁶ is below that hit's odds.
  3. RevealWhen the card is caught (or recalled), the secret is published. Your browser recomputes every roll in the full throw log.

The HMAC key is the raw secret bytes; the full 256-bit digest is read as an integer and divided by 2²⁵⁶. Each release's odds are fixed when it goes out and shown with its throw log.

How it works from launch

Every trade of $COVE pays creator fees. Every few minutes we claim them on-chain and pour them into the island.

⚡

Fees spawn golden balls

Each claim scatters golden balls across the island. Walk over one to put it in your bag. First come, first served.

🃏

A real card roams free

The rest fills a USDC vault. At $10 we buy a real graded card and release it as a creature wandering the island.

🎯

Hit it with golden balls

Every hit is a roll. Odds start at 0.4% and climb +0.012 pts with every miss, up to 25%. Anyone's hit can be the one.

🎁

Catch it, claim it

Land the winning throw and paste any Solana wallet. No signature needed. The tokenized card is sent straight to you.

📖

Pink orbs are free and unlimited

Throw them at the island's critters to fill your dex: 30 island species plus three extensions, Ocean (they swim offshore), Sky (they land for a few seconds) and Cave (deep in the crystal mountain). Legendaries only show up during rare timed events. The first trainer to complete everything wins a mystery Master card.

🔒

Provably fair

We can't pick the winner, and you can check it yourself.

  1. Commit. When a card is released the server draws a random 32-byte secret and publishes sha256(secret), also written in an on-chain memo.
  2. Roll. Throw #n (index i = n − 1) wins if HMAC-SHA256(secret, "cardId:i"), read as a number in [0,1), is below that throw's odds.
  3. Reveal. After the catch the secret is published. Your browser re-computes every roll; nothing is trusted.

Number format: the first 13 hex characters (52 bits) of the HMAC digest divided by 252. The HMAC key is the raw secret bytes.
